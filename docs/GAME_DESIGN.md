# The Adventure Book — Game & Visual Direction v0.1

## North star

**Real life is the adventure. The game is its beautiful record.** Every action should make families more excited to do meaningful things together, not trapped by a daily login. A completed quest should immediately create a cherished story and a tangible change in the game world.

## Identity

- World: **Evergrove**, an intimate, magical forest clearing that grows into an evolving family landscape.
- UI: enchanted journal, enamel badges, paper labels, copper ornaments, subtle dew and starlight.
- Mood: warm, curious, nostalgic, playful, inclusive of adults and children. Minimal fantasy combat; the family is the hero.
- Camera: faux-isometric 2.5D. Parallax in foreground/background, layered vector rigging, atmospheric day-night and particle lights. Future characters should receive multi-part skeletal animation rather than frame-swapped icons.
- Modes: bright magical morning, cozy evening, moonlit Evergrove, weather and seasonal themes. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and offer independent animation/sound toggles.

## Fundamental loop

1. Choose or invent an adventure (place, people, budget, duration).
2. Schedule it if appropriate and invite participants.
3. Do it together, without forcing anyone to stare at a screen.
4. Mark it complete and record words/photos/voice.
5. Earn personal XP, cooperative family XP and cosmetic resources.
6. Add a permanent memory page; unlock cosmetic gear/world decorations.
7. Return to new ideas, traditions and family-history moments.

## Five gameplay pillars

**Adventure Board:** Filter by cost, energy, weather, age suitability, length and interests. Collaborative quest proposals and voting. Recurring family traditions.

**Family Chronicle:** Every memory is editable, independent of quests, ordered by year/volume, exportable, and optionally private to selected family members. Placeholders must never replace real-life history. Memory anniversaries should be opt-in and gentle.

**Crew:** Adult accounts, child-managed profiles with parental roles, optional extended relatives. Individual levels are personal, not competitive; avatars, gear and collectibles express personality.

**Living World:** Quest types map to location decorations. Exploration earns trail banners, crafting earns garden elements, cooking earns cottage objects, togetherness earns lamps/hearth effects. No indispensable unlock requires paid content.

**Calendar:** Plan time together, repeat traditions, celebrate birthdays, and turn upcoming activities into shared plans. Missing a day causes no penalties.

## Reward system proposals

- Complete a typical quest: +50–90 XP and +10–25 tokens.
- Original memory: +10 XP, +3 tokens (gentle incentive; rate limit later for anti-spam).
- New family profile: no XP. Child participation can be recognized without encouraging mass invitations.
- Family levels unlock world cosmetic districts, paper textures, animated stickers, and storybook scenes.
- No pay-to-win, forced streaks, leaderboards between children, or punishments for inactivity.

## Animation behavior

| Surface | Prototype | Target production |
|---|---|---|
| Homebase | Layered SVG island, atmospheric parallax, idle forest life | Art-directed 2.5D scene, camera push/pan, seasonal cycles |
| Character | Tap-to-walk, leg gait, bob, hair/eyes/arm animations, CSS profile rig | Multi-part rig, idle/walk/celebrate/emote states, outfit layers |
| Menus | Animated page entrances, hover states, rising modals | Shared menu choreography, authored microinteractions, transitions |
| Chronicle | Tactile paper layout and page content navigation | Actual curved paper page turns, sticker placement, handwriting reveal |
| Rewards | Particles, tokens, badge toasts, synthesized chime | Bespoke reward animations, motion motifs, character celebrations |
| Audio | Opt-in synthesized taps and success chord | Licensed/owned adaptive music and ambient soundscape |

## Future roadmap design priorities

1. Establish memorable characters and one exceptional world, not ten generic screens.
2. Make every game loop task possible on a phone in under 30 seconds, except writing/editing memories.
3. Make the Chronicle feel like a family heirloom, including meaningful permanent export.
4. Build parental oversight, child-safe identity and tenant separation before inviting unrelated families.
5. Provide data deletion, export and off-site backup at launch of the cloud service.

All names, levels, quests, and visual elements in this document are working designs and can be changed through later development.
